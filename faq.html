Do I pay online?

No. SwiftSupply uses order requests rather than online payment. Submit your cart and we will follow up to confirm the order and final amount.

Does placing an order reserve the items?

Your order request checks and deducts available inventory when it is successfully submitted. We will still confirm the order afterward.

Can I order more than one product?

Yes. Add everything you want to your cart and submit the whole cart as one order.

How do I track an order?

Use the order number and private tracking code from your confirmation email or tracking link on the Track Order page.

What happens if an order is cancelled?

Cancelled orders return their ordered quantities to inventory automatically.

Can I ask for a product that is not listed?

Yes. Use the Product Requests page and SwiftSupply can consider adding it.

Can I get a stock alert?

Yes. Choose a brand on the Stock Alerts page and enter your email.

How do Power Washing quotes work?

Send the job details through the Power Washing quote form. SwiftSupply reviews the request first, follows up if more information is needed, and confirms the quote and scheduling before anything is booked.

What can Power Washing clean?

The current request options include driveways, sidewalks, patios, outdoor areas, trash cans, fences, exterior walls, multiple areas, and other exterior surfaces that can be reviewed case-by-case.
