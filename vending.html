    SSVM · SWIFTSUPPLY VENDING

    Vending is its own branch.

    SSVM is the vending-machine side of SwiftSupply. It focuses on machine locations, stocked inventory, convenient access, and growing a network of machines over time.

    WHAT SSVM DOES

    Built around machines.

    SSVM is separate from the SS preorder-and-delivery branch. If a customer wants to preorder drinks or snacks directly, that goes through SS Shop. SSVM is specifically for vending operations.

    01

    Machine locations

    SSVM grows by placing vending machines where convenient access makes sense.

    02

    Machine inventory

    Products stocked inside SSVM machines are managed as part of the vending operation.

    03

    Expansion

    Revenue from across SwiftSupply can help SSVM reach more machines and better locations over time.

    CURRENT DIRECTION
