NOTICES

Policies & notices.

Simple information about how SwiftSupply handles orders and this website.

Order requests

Submitting a cart is a request, not an online payment or guaranteed pickup appointment. SwiftSupply will review the request and confirm the order.

Inventory

Inventory is live and may change quickly. A successful order submission reduces available stock, while cancelled orders return stock.

Pricing

Prices and bundle offers shown on the site can change. The order confirmation and follow-up from SwiftSupply are used to confirm the final amount.

Privacy

Information submitted through orders, support, requests, and alerts is used to respond to those requests and operate SwiftSupply. Do not submit sensitive information through the site.

Website use

SwiftSupply is a small student-run project. Website availability, product availability, and response times are not guaranteed.

Contact

If you need help with an order or website issue, use the Support page.
